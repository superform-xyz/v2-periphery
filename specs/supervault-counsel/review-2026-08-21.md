## Verdict

**No P0/P1 contract defect in any review.** The core mechanics — state machine (half-open window,
derived Ready/Expired, terminal absorption), CEI, payload isolation across the shared struct,
sweep safety, session-key revival handling, and every cross-contract auth/unit/timelock
assumption — were independently verified against callee source. The risk mass is operational.
